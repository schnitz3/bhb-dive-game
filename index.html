How to play

  1. 1Swim right. The current pushes back, so keep kicking.
  2. 2Bob's air runs out fast. Hold hands and Lisa shares hers, so it lasts.
  3. 3The Goby Fish has perfect eyesight. Catch him for fresh air.
  4. 4The Candy Cane Shrimp is tiny but strong. Catch him for a burst through anything.
  5. 5Catch both within a few seconds for a Symbiosis bonus. That is the whole story.
  6. 6Bubble curtains and seaweed only stop you when you are apart.
  7. 7A jellyfish stings either way. When the screen shakes, swim clear of it.

Arrow keys or W A S D to swim. Hold Space to hold hands. P pauses, M mutes.

Drag on the left to swim. Hold on the right to hold hands.
